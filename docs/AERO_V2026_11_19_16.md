# v2026.11.19-16 - aircraft cruise power

## Behavior
- Removed the fixed18m/s cruise target during clear-route transit, patrol and attack legs. The pilot holds the configured accelerate input; each aircraft's engine thrust and drag determine the attainable speed. No velocity is injected and no fixed maximum speed is invented.
- Far return legs also use maximum power. Within1km of the deck, approach speed control remains.
- Escort retains speed matching instead of forcing maximum power. Removed the18m/s cap; its target is the tracked contact speed +4.4m/s, with a7m/s minimum. An aircraft slower than the escort target can only pursue at its own available power.
- Replaced the fixed80m/s contact-motion rejection threshold with a comparison to the target's physical speed. Teleport discontinuities and nonfinite observations are still rejected.
- Controlled-speed flight remains for training, landing alignment, final approach, orbiting at a destination, large heading errors (>60 degrees), extreme bank (>50 degrees), traffic and blocked-route avoidance. Takeoff and touchdown logic are unchanged.
- Trace reports cruise_full_power / maximum_cruise_power; controlled modes include target_forward_speed_mps.
